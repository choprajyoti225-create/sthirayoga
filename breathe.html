Pranayama

Change the breath, change the state.

Breathing is the one part of your nervous system you can steer on purpose. Pick a practice, press begin and follow the orb.

Ready

16

second cycle

Time
0:00
Rounds
0
Pattern
4·4·4·4

How to

  1. 1.Sit upright with both feet on the floor.
  2. 2.Breathe in through the nose for a count of four.
  3. 3.Hold gently — no strain in the throat.
  4. 4.Exhale slowly for four, then pause empty for four.

When to use it

Before a presentation, after a stressful email, or whenever you need to feel level.

Breathe gently and never strain. If you feel dizzy or anxious, stop and return to natural breathing. Skip breath-holds if you are pregnant or have heart or respiratory conditions.
