Tadasana

Mountain

tada — mountain

The blueprint for every standing pose. It looks like simply standing, but done well it is an active, quietly alert shape.

T
Hold5–8 breaths

How to do it

  1. 1

    Stand with feet hip-width apart, second toes pointing forward.

  2. 2

    Lift and spread your toes, then lay them back down to widen the base.

  3. 3

    Draw the thighs up gently and let the tailbone lengthen toward the floor.

  4. 4

    Stack ribs over pelvis and shoulders over ribs; let the arms hang with palms facing forward.

  5. 5

    Lengthen through the crown of the head and soften the jaw.

Focus:PostureFeetCore

Why practise it

  • Builds awareness of everyday posture
  • Strengthens thighs, knees and ankles
  • Gives a calm, grounded starting point for practice

Make it gentler

Stand with your back against a wall to feel where your head, shoulder blades and pelvis line up.

Take care

If you feel light-headed, widen your stance and keep a slight bend in the knees.
